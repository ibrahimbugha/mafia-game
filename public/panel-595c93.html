🔑 كيف أغيّر كلمة المرور؟ · How do I change the password?
  1. Render → your service → Environment → Add Environment Variable
  2. Key: ADMIN_KEY · Value: a long password (8+ chars)
  3. Save. The service restarts. The new password replaces the old one (the old one stops working).
